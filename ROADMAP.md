# Roadmap

Hanzi Journey is intentionally starting with a compact HSK 1 learning loop. This roadmap is directional; it does not claim a release date.

## Near-term

- Review and improve the starter vocabulary and sentence examples with learner feedback.
- Add migration/seed quality checks and more browser coverage when a shared test database is available.
- Refine onboarding, account recovery, and accessible keyboard behavior.
- Add additional HSK 1 lessons where they improve topic coverage.

## Future curriculum

- HSK 2 and HSK 3 content packs.
- More listening variations and contextual sentence practice.
- A richer review scheduler, potentially FSRS, after enough usage data and evaluation.

## Possible future platform ideas

An AI tutor, chatbot, social features, multiplayer learning, payments or subscriptions, a teacher dashboard, a mobile app, complex speech recognition, leaderboards, and advanced recommendations are outside the first release. They may be explored later if they support the learning goal without making the core experience harder to maintain.
