# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community where people can contribute regardless of experience, identity, background, or language ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and patient.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and respect.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not a person’s worth or identity.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal information without consent.
- Keep discussions focused on learning, accessibility, and the project’s goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, threats, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. Report concerns privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact a repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed discreetly and fairly.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
